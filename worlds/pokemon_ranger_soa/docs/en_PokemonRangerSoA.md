### Fixes
These are fixes/changes to the game to allow randomization to function properly without negatively affecting the gameplay experience.

| What                                                                                                                                                              | When       |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Patched that destroying the red gigaremo before triggering events in the top room of Marine cave does not hard softlock the game                                  | Mission 2  |
| Mimi will now accept any Happiny. A singular Happiny will now be enough to have her leave.                                                                        | Mission 3  |
| Patched that any Blastoise can be used to put out the forest fire                                                                                                 | Mission 3  |
| Extended the event trigger of being pushed back by the Elekid wall in Pueltown-center, it now extends to behind it to prevent getting softlocked in Pueltown-west | Mission 4  |
| Patched a very niche softlock by changing the top right trampoline plant in the first room of Peril Cliffs to throw you down instead of to the top left.          | Mission 6  |
| When field moves are unlock items, the Cargo Ship can not be entered before unlocking all required field moves                                                    | Mission 8  |
| When field moves are unlock items, you are prevented from jumping down the first hole in Chrome Ruins                                                             | Mission 9  |
| Patched that any Skuntank can be used to use stink in Chroma Ruins                                                                                                | Mission 9  |
| Patched that Capt. Wail shows up at Sailor Inn during mission 13                                                                                                  | Mission 13 |
